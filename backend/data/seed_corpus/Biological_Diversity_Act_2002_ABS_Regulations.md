# Biological Diversity Act, 2002 & Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Regulations

## Section 3 - Certain Persons not to Undertake Biodiversity-Related Activities without Approval
No person who is not a citizen of India, or a body corporate, association or organization not incorporated or registered in India, or having non-Indian participation in its share capital or management, shall obtain any biological resource occurring in India or knowledge associated thereto for research or for commercial utilization or for bio-survey and bio-utilization without previous approval of the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA).

## Section 6 - Application for Intellectual Property Rights
1. No person shall apply for any intellectual property right, by whatever name called, in or outside India for any invention based on any research or information on a biological resource obtained from India without obtaining the previous approval of the National Biodiversity Authority before making such application.
2. In cases where patent applications are filed, the approval of the National Biodiversity Authority may be obtained after filing the application for the patent but before the grant of the patent by the patent authority.

## Section 21 - Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Mechanism
1. The National Biodiversity Authority while granting approval under Section 19 or Section 20 shall ensure that the terms and conditions determine equitable sharing of benefits arising out of the use of accessed biological resources, their by-products, innovations and practices associated with their use and applications and knowledge relating thereto in accordance with mutually agreed terms between the applicant, the local bodies concerned, and the benefit claimers.
2. Benefit sharing may involve monetary compensation (up to 0.1% to 0.5% of ex-factory sale price, or 2% to 5% of gross sales value), transfer of technology, joint research and development, or support to local biodiversity management committees (BMCs).
3. Commercial utilization of Ayurvedic herbs such as Withania somnifera (Ashwagandha), Commiphora mukul (Guggulu), and Bacopa monnieri (Brahmi) by foreign entities or Indian companies with foreign equity requires compliance with NBA approval and ABS obligations prior to commercial launch or export.

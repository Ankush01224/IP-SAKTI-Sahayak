# Ministry of Ayush Export Guidelines, Quality Certification & WHO-GMP Standards

## Chapter 1 - Ayush Mark Voluntary Certification Scheme
To enhance consumer confidence and facilitate international trade in Ayurvedic products, the Ministry of Ayush, in collaboration with the Quality Council of India (QCI), operates the voluntary Ayush Mark Certification Scheme:
1. **Ayush Standard Mark**: Confirms that the product strictly complies with the statutory domestic quality standards prescribed in the Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API) and Schedule T Good Manufacturing Practices.
2. **Ayush Premium Mark**: Designed for export competitiveness. Certifies compliance with stringent international norms, including WHO Good Manufacturing Practices (WHO-GMP) for Herbal Medicines and compliance with importing country regulatory limits for heavy metals, microbial load, pesticide residues, and aflatoxins.

## Chapter 2 - Mandatory Contaminant Permissible Thresholds
All Ayurvedic formulations packaged for export must be tested in NABL-accredited or Ministry of Ayush-approved drug testing laboratories and satisfy the following limits:
1. **Heavy Metal Permissible Limits**:
   - Lead (Pb): Maximum 10.0 ppm (mg/kg)
   - Cadmium (Cd): Maximum 0.3 ppm (mg/kg)
   - Arsenic (As): Maximum 3.0 ppm (mg/kg)
   - Mercury (Hg): Maximum 1.0 ppm (mg/kg)
2. **Microbial Contamination Limits**:
   - Total Aerobic Microbial Count (TAMC): Maximum 10^5 CFU/g for herbal materials.
   - Total Combined Yeast and Mold Count (TYMC): Maximum 10^3 CFU/g.
   - Escherichia coli: Absent in 1g.
   - Salmonella spp.: Absent in 10g.
   - Staphylococcus aureus: Absent in 1g.
3. **Aflatoxins**:
   - Aflatoxin B1: Maximum 2.0 mcg/kg (ppb)
   - Total Aflatoxins (B1 + B2 + G1 + G2): Maximum 4.0 mcg/kg (ppb).

## Chapter 3 - Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (CoPP)
Under the WHO Certification Scheme on the Quality of Pharmaceutical Products Moving in International Commerce, the State Licensing Authority, countersigned by the Central Ayush regulator, issues a WHO-GMP Certificate and Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (CoPP) valid for 3 years, required by importing health agencies across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and Latin America.
